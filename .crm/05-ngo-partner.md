# 05 — NGO / Institutional Partner
**Trigger:** Manual — partnership inquiry or outreach · Channel: Email · Tone: formal, mission-aligned

---

## CONTEXT

For environmental NGOs, research institutions, universities, bilateral development
agencies, or corporate CSR programmes exploring partnership with SCD Hub / PON INK.
This letter is more formal than community-facing ones. It should be customised
per recipient — the template provides structure, not a mail-merge.

Key offer for institutions:
- Eco-ops data on-chain — tamper-evident, community-owned, auditable
- ART token disbursement to field teams as recognition currency
- Exolocation deeds as a way to anchor field sites virtually
- Custom airdrop campaigns for your beneficiaries — no platform fee on the airdrop design
- Transparent revenue: 99% to field teams; 0.75% hardware fund; 0.25% platform

---

## EMAIL

**Subject:** `Partnership inquiry — SCD Hub · Sustainable community data infrastructure`

```
Dear {{contact_name}},

I am writing on behalf of the SCD Hub — a US-based non-profit building
open-source digital infrastructure for field communities working on water,
food, energy, and environmental monitoring.

---

WHAT WE BUILD

PON INK is our operations portal. It connects community artists, field workers,
and development practitioners through a shared blockchain record. Everything
a participant does — a water quality measurement, a crop map, a cultural
performance — is permanently and tamper-evidently recorded on-chain.

The platform is built on three principles:
1. Communities own their data — no lock-in, no proprietary format, GPL v3
2. Field workers keep 99% of any value their work generates on the platform
3. The infrastructure is open — any NGO or research partner can read and
   export data through our API with participant consent

---

HOW A PARTNERSHIP MIGHT WORK

For your field teams or beneficiaries, we could offer:

· Exolocation deeds — virtual property anchored to real field sites,
  used as a unique identifier for community-based monitoring locations

· ART token disbursement — recognition tokens awarded to field workers
  for verified eco-ops submissions; resellable, but also holdable as
  community credit

· Custom airdrop campaigns — you design the bundle; we handle the
  on-chain distribution; your beneficiaries receive settlement objects,
  POAPs, and participation credentials at no platform charge

· Co-authored data layers — your Earth-side dataset linked to Exotopia
  virtual settlements, making field sites discoverable internationally

---

THE ECONOMIC MODEL

The platform takes 0.25% of transactions. The 0.75% Community Hardware Fund
goes directly to WATSAN equipment and field infrastructure. Both figures are
visible on every transaction before confirmation and verifiable on-chain.

For partnership-funded campaigns (where you cover the gas fees), the
standard split can be configured differently through a custom contract.

---

WE WOULD LIKE TO KNOW MORE ABOUT

- Your current field data infrastructure and pain points
- Which communities or regions you are working with
- Whether your team has existing blockchain / digital asset capability
- What a pilot programme of 20-50 participants might look like for you

---
```

## NOTES FOR SENDER

- Heavily personalise: reference the NGO's known programmes, regions, and partners
- Avoid blockchain jargon — say "permanent digital record" not "NFT" in opening paragraphs
- If they are already familiar with Web3: lean into the on-chain verifiability angle
- Do not mention $SUNLIGHT in the first letter to institutional contacts — it reads as speculative
- Legal spec (SPEC_LEGAL.MD): GDPR/NDPA compliance, data sovereignty, open-source licensing — mention these explicitly for European or Kenyan contacts
